The invitation

कुछ शामें देखी नहीं जातीं।
महसूस की जाती हैं।

India has never experienced poetry in silence.

We have gathered around it. We have laughed at a perfectly timed line, applauded a fearless thought, repeated a sher to someone we love and carried a favourite verse home with us.

The Navbharat Times Kavi Sammelan Series brings that tradition to the contemporary auditorium across five cities.

“A mehfil made for the city—and remembered by everyone in the room.”
