Lucknow Edition

Grand Shaam-e-Awadh

Jahan Lucknow ki tehzeeb milti hai Hindi kavya ki taaqat se.

Lucknow, one of India's most culturally rich Hindi-speaking cities. Home to tehzeeb, poetry, music, humour, cuisine and refined public life. A city where language is art, and every gathering carries the grace of a tradition centuries old.

Grand Shaam-e-Awadh celebrates this cultural soul through a dignified, premium and family-friendly evening, where prominent citizens, cultural voices, professionals and literature lovers come together for an unforgettable mehfil.

Celebrating Lucknow's language, laughter, legacy and leadership.

The Format

Language becomes experience.

Kavi Sammelan is one of India's most loved live cultural formats. It brings humour, satire, patriotism, emotion, geet, ghazal and social commentary together on a single stage, creating an evening of deep emotional connection with the audience.

In an age of fleeting digital interactions, this is a rare opportunity to experience the power of live words, shared laughter, and collective cultural pride.

  • High-attention environment through poetry and humour
  • Family-friendly, dignified cultural gathering
  • Premium Hindi-speaking audience connect
  • Local city relevance with digital scalability
  • Strong emotional recall beyond conventional formats

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Grand Shaam-e-Awadh?

Grand Shaam-e-Awadh is the Lucknow edition of the Navbharat Times Kavi Sammelan Series, a premier cultural evening featuring Hindi poetry performances, Lucknow cultural acts, and the Lucknow Ratna Samman felicitation ceremony.

When and where is the event?

The event is scheduled for 25 October 2026 in Lucknow. The specific venue will be announced closer to the date and is subject to finalisation.

How can I register for the event?

You can register your interest through the registration form on this website. Entry is subject to registration confirmation, venue capacity and organiser approval.

What is the Lucknow Ratna Samman?

It is a felicitation ceremony honouring Lucknow's finest achievers across categories including Business, Healthcare, Education, Arts & Culture, Literature, Social Impact, Women Leadership and Young Achievers.

Can brands and organisations partner with this event?

Yes. Multiple partnership opportunities are available for brands looking to engage with premium Hindi-speaking audiences through a trusted cultural platform. Please reach out via the partnership enquiry form or contact our team.

What kind of audience will attend?

The event brings together a curated mix of city personalities, industrialists, business families, professionals, educators, cultural figures, literature lovers and premium family audiences, not a generic crowd-led event.

Will the event be streamed online?

Select content from the event may be published across digital and social platforms after the event. Details will be shared closer to the date.
