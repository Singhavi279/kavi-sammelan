Grand Shaam-e-Awadh | Lucknow Edition

Jahan Lucknow ki tehzeeb milti hai Hindi kavya ki taaqat se.

Lucknow sirf ek shahar nahi, ek andaaz hai. Yahan ki tehzeeb, adab, zubaan, shayari, humour aur public life ne hamesha Hindi-Urdu culture ko ek khaas pehchaan di hai.

Grand Shaam-e-Awadh isi cultural soul ko celebrate karega – ek dignified, premium aur family-friendly Kavi Sammelan evening ke through, jahan Lucknow ke prominent citizens, cultural voices, policymakers, industrialists, professionals aur literature lovers ek saath ek yaadgaar mehfil ka hissa banenge.

Language becomes experience.

Kavi Sammelan India ke sabse loved live cultural formats mein se ek hai. Yeh humour, satire, patriotism, emotion, geet, ghazal aur social commentary ko ek saath lekar aata hai. Isliye yeh sirf entertainment nahi, balki audience ke saath emotional connect banata hai.

Aaj jab zyada brand conversations digital ya transactional ho chuki hain, Kavi Sammelan ek rare opportunity deta hai – jahan brands culture, families, city pride aur premium Hindi-speaking audiences ke saath meaningful association bana sakte hain.

  • Humour aur poetry ke through high attention environment
  • Family-friendly cultural gathering
  • Premium Hindi-speaking audience connect
  • Local city relevance with digital scalability
  • Strong emotional recall beyond conventional advertising
